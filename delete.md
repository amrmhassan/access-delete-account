# Access to Citizenship - Access California Services (AccessCal)

How to delete your Access to Citizenship account and the data linked to it.

## Delete your account in the app
1. Open the app and go to the Settings page
1. Click on 'Delete My Account' button
1. Press 'Delete' button

## Request deletion without the app
Send an email to **amrmhassanwork@gmail.com** from the email address you use in the app, with the subject "Delete my Access to Citizenship account". We will delete your account and its data within 30 days of your request.

## Data that is deleted
- Your account and profile: name, email address, profile photo and user ID
- Your study progress, test results, mock interview history and reminder settings
- Your notification token

## Data that is kept
- Payment records for paid interviews are kept by our payment processor (Stripe) for as long as the law requires.
- Anonymous crash and diagnostic logs (Firebase) are kept for up to 90 days.

Appointment dates, document checklists and eligibility answers are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.
